Summer 2026 Kids Camp

Real tennis.
Real fun.

The Court & Cast Kids Tennis Camp is a morning program for children ages 8–14 at the Falmouth High School courts. Small groups, certified coaching, and a genuine love of the game.

"The goal isn't perfect technique — it's kids who can't wait to come back."
🎾

Age-appropriate groups. Two levels — Mini Orange (8–10) and Green & Yellow (11–14) — each with their own equipment, court size, and curriculum.

👩‍🏫

PTR Certified coaching. Coach Amy is PTR Certified and teaches with a player-first philosophy. Small groups mean every kid gets real attention and real feedback.

☀️🌧️

Rain or shine — no cancellations. Sessions run regardless of weather. Pack a light layer and don't worry about it.

🏖️

Done by noon every day. Morning sessions wrap by 11:45 AM. Plenty of beach time left for the whole family.

⛵

Perfect for visiting families. On Cape Cod for a week or two? Book a session and make it count. No long-term commitment required.

Your Coach

Meet
Coach Amy.

Cape Cod local. Certified coach. Licensed captain. Amy brings the same passion to the tennis court that she brings to the water.

🎾

⚓

📍
Cape Cod LocalBased in Falmouth · Deep community roots

Amy's coaching philosophy is simple: restore confidence before correcting technique. Adult returning players retain knowledge but motor patterns drift — so sessions start with success and build from there.

With kids, the goal isn't perfect form. It's getting them to love the sport. Small groups mean every child gets genuine attention, real feedback, and plenty of time hitting balls.

Whether your child is picking up a racket for the first time or working on match tactics, they'll leave every session having learned something and had fun doing it.
